# **App Name**: Tohfa Atelier

## Core Features:

- Interactive Entry Experience: A grand splash page displaying the brand's unique, textured logo prominently, inviting user interaction to proceed to the main site.
- Luxurious Product Gallery: An elegant, shareable public interface showcasing pottery products with high-quality images, names, selectable color variants, prices in TND, and rich descriptive paragraphs.
- Brand Narrative Section: A dedicated page detailing the 'Tohfa' brand story (handcrafted pottery, plates, cups), complete with Instagram links, contact phone number, and a custom footer ('By Azizos').
- Secure Administrative Panel: A password-protected login portal enabling administrators to access a private section for managing all site content and product inventory.
- Intuitive Product Management Tool: An admin-exclusive tool allowing for seamless uploading of new product images from a device gallery, defining product attributes (name, selectable colors, TND price), and adding detailed descriptions.
- Responsive Cross-Device Access: Ensures that both the public-facing product gallery and the admin features are fully functional and beautifully rendered across a wide range of devices, including mobile phones and desktops.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color Inspiration: Drawing from the earthy tones of handcrafted pottery, rich clay, and the desire for a luxurious, warm aesthetic. A light color scheme is selected.
- Primary Color: A deep, warm clay brown, evoking artisanal craftsmanship and elegance. HSL(25, 45%, 28%) converted to RGB hex: #6C4327.
- Background Color: A soft, inviting beige, which is a heavily desaturated variant of the primary hue, providing a luxurious canvas. HSL(25, 15%, 96%) converted to RGB hex: #F9F4F0.
- Accent Color: A rich caramel-gold hue, analogous to the primary brown, designed to highlight key elements and add a sophisticated gleam. HSL(55, 65%, 55%) converted to RGB hex: #D0A343.
- Animated background elements will feature various shades of deep chocolate brown and caramel, providing dynamic, blurred texture.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif chosen for its elegant and high-end appeal. Body text font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif, offering excellent readability and a contemporary warmth that complements the headline style.
- Icons will be minimalist and refined, with subtle textual hints or an organic, ceramic-like finish to resonate with the 'rough' aesthetic of the brand's logo and pottery theme. Buttons will possess a 'clay' tactile feel.
- The initial design will feature a centrally positioned, commanding logo, elegantly transitioning to a compact version in a top-centered navigation bar. The overall layout will prioritize spaciousness, clear product focus, and mobile-first responsiveness for all content.
- Subtle, slow-moving blurred 'halo' or 'bubble' shapes in deep brown and caramel tones will create an atmospheric background. Smooth and luxurious transitions will be applied between the splash screen and main site sections, enhancing user engagement.
